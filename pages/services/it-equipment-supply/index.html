IT Equipment Supply

The Capability We Build

Twenty-two services covering the whole life of a hardware estate — from the first quotation through to the disposal certificate.

Vendor-Neutral by Default

We buy across competing vendors rather than reselling one of them, so the specification answers to your requirement instead of to a distribution agreement. That applies to laptops and servers the same as it does to switches, firewalls and the licences that run on them.

Owned From Purchase Order to Disposal Certificate

Sourcing is the easy part. We image and configure before delivery, install and cable on site, tag every unit into an asset register, carry the warranty and RMA chasing, plan the refresh — and when it is finally retired, erase the media and recycle it through a licensed route.
