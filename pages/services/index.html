How We Work

Our OSSF Framework

Every security engagement runs through the same eight stages, from first measuring the threat landscape to keeping the business running after an incident.

  1. 01

    Threat Landscape

    Finding and measuring the threat landscape.

  2. 02

    Gap Analysis

    Finding and filling the gaps in infrastructure security.

  3. 03

    Secure Architecture

    Designing a fully customised and redundant architecture.

  4. 04

    Implementation

    Orchestration of the planned architecture.

  5. 05

    Reactive Maintenance

    The implemented SOC must adhere to reactive maintenance.

  6. 06

    Proactive Maintenance

    The implemented SOC must assure proactive maintenance.

  7. 07

    Disaster Recovery

    Effective disaster recovery planning and implementation.

  8. 08

    Continuity

    Continuation of infrastructure and business after an incident.
